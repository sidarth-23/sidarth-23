### 🧑‍💻 About Me

I'm **Sidarth G**, a Full Stack Developer at **Truvanta** building SaaS products, internal tools, CMS platforms, and deployment workflows. I work across the stack — from TypeScript and React on the frontend to Node.js, Go, and PostgreSQL on the backend — and I care about developer experience, reliable systems, and shipping fast.

Previously at **TeachEdison**, I built [Bluebook Insights](https://www.elecsavers.com) from scratch, contributed to an open-source LMS, and helped grow site traffic by 20% while keeping uptime above 98%.

I enjoy converting operational pain points into product features, standardizing APIs with OpenAPI, and making teams move faster through better tooling and CI/CD.
